# Performance Efficiency

Degree to which a product performs its functions within specified time and throughput parameters and is efficient in the use of resources (such as CPU, memory, storage, network devices, energy, materials...) under specified conditions.

## Sub-characteristics

### Time behaviour

Degree to which the response time and throughput rates of a product or system meet requirements.

### Resource utilization

Degree to which the amounts and types of resources used by a product or system meet requirements.

### Capacity

Degree to which the maximum limits of a product or system parameter meet requirements.
